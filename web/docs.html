NATIVE VITA / PLAYER FIELD GUIDE

Five screens.
One offline-first player log.

The native 00.10 build is a local gaming companion, not a replacement launcher. It browses the bundled catalog, records your own collection state and keeps three player profiles in the app's data area. It does not scan installed titles, launch games, inspect licenses or touch save data.
